Shipping and Delivery Policy

Standard shipping within India takes 3-5 business days from the date of dispatch. Express shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee and takes 1-2 business days.

Orders are usually dispatched within 24 hours of being placed, except on Sundays and public holidays. Once dispatched, you will receive a tracking link via email and SMS.

International shipping is available to over 40 countries. International orders typically take 7-14 business days depending on customs processing in the destination country. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their local authorities.

If your order has not arrived within the expected window, please check the tracking link first. If tracking shows no movement for more than 3 days, contact support and we will investigate with the courier partner. In rare cases of lost packages, we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund after a 10 business day investigation period with the courier.

We currently ship using BlueDart, Delhivery, and India Post depending on your pincode and item weight. You cannot choose your courier partner manually, but you can request express shipping to reduce delivery time.

Delivery attempts are made up to 3 times. If all three attempts fail, the package is returned to our warehouse and you will need to contact support to reschedule delivery or request a refund.
