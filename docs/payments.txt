Payments FAQ

We accept credit cards, debit cards, UPI, net banking, and popular wallets for domestic orders. For international orders, we accept PayPal and major international credit cards.

If a payment fails but the amount is deducted from your account, it is usually auto-reversed by your bank within 5-7 business days. If it is not reversed after 7 days, please contact support with your transaction ID and we will help you follow up with the payment gateway.

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders under Rs.5000 in most serviceable pincodes. A small COD handling fee of Rs.30 applies. COD is not available for international orders or orders above Rs.5000.

EMI options are available on select credit cards for orders above Rs.3000, shown at checkout if your card is eligible. EMI processing fees, if any, are charged by your bank, not by us.

All payments are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways. We do not store your full card details on our servers at any point.

If you were charged twice for the same order due to a technical error, please contact support immediately with both transaction IDs. Duplicate charges are refunded within 3-5 business days after verification.
