Return and Refund Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with an item, you can return it within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.

To be eligible for a return, the item must be unused, in its original condition, and in the original packaging with all tags attached. Items marked as final sale, personal care items, and undergarments are not eligible for return.

If your item arrived damaged or defective, please contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. In these cases, we cover the return shipping cost and process a full refund or replacement, whichever you prefer, without requiring you to send the item back first in most cases.

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. For damaged item claims, refunds are typically issued within 2-3 business days once photos are verified.

If you paid via UPI or net banking, refunds may take an additional 2-3 days to reflect in your bank statement due to bank processing times. Store credit refunds, if chosen, are instant.

We do not currently offer exchanges directly; if you'd like a different size or color, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order separately.
